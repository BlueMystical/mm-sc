# Mine Market SC
A marketplace where Star Citizen miners publish their ore inventories and find buyers and sellers.
# Features
- Browse offers in Explore, no account needed.
- Log in with Discord to sell or buy.
- Create an inventory, add your ores and share its link.
- Manage requests in Orders, contact the other party on Discord and rate them when it is done.
